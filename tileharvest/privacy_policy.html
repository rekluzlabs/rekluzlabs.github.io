Privacy Policy for TileHarvest Last updated: August 19, 2026 TileHarvest is developed by Rekluz Labs. We take your privacy seriously, and our approach is straightforward: we don’t collect, sell, or share your personal information. The app is designed to run entirely on your device, and we have no access to your data. Summary TileHarvest operates offline by default. We don’t require accounts, use cloud sync, or employ any tracking, analytics, or advertising. The only exception is an optional Google Photos integration, detailed below. Data Collection and Processing We collect no personal data. No sign-ups, no emails, no user profiles. All image processing occurs locally on your device. We don’t track usage or monitor app activity. Google Photos Integration (Optional) If you choose to enable it, TileHarvest allows you to search and select images from your Google Photos library. Here’s how it works: This feature is disabled by default and requires your explicit consent. You select specific photos through Google’s official interface. Only the images you choose are downloaded to your device. We never access your full library or retain any data. All selected images are processed locally. This feature follows Google’s Privacy Policy and adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements. Permissions TileHarvest may request device permissions (such as storage access) solely to enable you to select images and save your work. These permissions are used strictly for their intended purpose, and no data collected through them leaves your device except as described above. Data Retention We don’t store any personal data on our servers. All app data—including selected images, project files, and settings—is stored exclusively on your device and remains under your control. Uninstalling the app removes this data, subject to your device’s normal behavior. Children’s Privacy TileHarvest does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. As we don’t collect personal data, we also don’t collect it from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Third-Party Services Apart from the optional Google Photos integration described above, TileHarvest does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-sharing services. Changes to This Policy We may revise this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. Updates will be posted with a revised “Last updated” date. Continued use of TileHarvest after such changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
