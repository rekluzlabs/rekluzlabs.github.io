Old photos, restored without ever leaving your phone.

MakoRevive repairs and colorizes damaged or faded photographs using on-device AI models. There's no upload step — the image never leaves your device, and Rekluz Labs never sees it.

In active development · Android

Restoration & colorization

Repairs scratches, fading, and damage, and can add color to black-and-white photographs — all through on-device models.

Nothing leaves the device

Processing happens locally. We do not receive, view, or store the photos you process — there is no server in the loop.

Export how you need it

Save restored photos in the format the destination actually needs — from everyday images to print-ready documents.

Good to know

Restoration is heavy work — for your phone too.

Restoring or colorizing a photo is computationally intensive. When you process an image, your device's processor works at or near full capacity for the duration of that task, so it's normal for your phone to become warm or to slow down briefly — similar to extended gaming or video editing. This isn't a sign that MakoRevive or your device is malfunctioning, and your device's built-in thermal safeguards handle it automatically.

Full details on device performance, content ownership, and warranty are covered in the Terms of Use.
