The four tools

One workflow, four specialized models.

Each tool targets a specific kind of damage rather than applying one generic filter to everything.

Denoise

Cleans up grainy photos taken in low light for a smooth, professional finish.

Colorize

Guesses natural-looking color for black-and-white photos, so they feel like they were taken yesterday.

Face Restoration

Targets blurry, pixelated, or damaged faces specifically, bringing back clarity while keeping the person's original look.

HD Upscaling

Turns small, low-quality images into large, sharp photos ready for printing.

Good to know

Restoration is heavy work — for your phone too.

To keep the initial download small, MakoRevive downloads its AI models the first time you open the app. Once those models are on your device, the app works entirely offline — restore photos anywhere, with no connection required.

Restoring or colorizing a photo is computationally intensive, so the app recommends Android 12 or higher. When you process an image, your device's processor works at or near full capacity for the duration of that task, so it's normal for your phone to become warm or to slow down briefly — similar to extended gaming or video editing. This isn't a sign that MakoRevive or your device is malfunctioning, and your device's built-in thermal safeguards handle it automatically.

Full details on device performance, content ownership, and warranty are covered in the Terms of Use.
