Every memory, kept in someone's own words.

Reminera is an offline family memory app. Record voices, photos, and videos, write a life story chapter by chapter, and bind it all into a book-style keepsake — without an account, a server, or a single upload.

Record once, keep forever

Photos, videos, and voice recordings are captured and stored directly on the device. Nothing is compressed for upload, because nothing is ever uploaded.

A life, in chapters

Biography sections and story entries build into a proper memoir, not a scattered camera roll — organized by person, by chapter, by year.

No servers, no accounts

There is no Reminera backend. Every family profile and recording lives in a local database that only exists on your device.

Why offline-first
"A family's memories shouldn't need a login. We just build the book around what's already on your phone."

Most memory-keeping apps ask you to hand your family's photos and stories to a cloud service in exchange for convenience. Reminera keeps that trade off the table entirely — it's a direct extension of the studio's mission statement.
