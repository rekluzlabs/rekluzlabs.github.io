The Studio

One developer. Every layer, one point of view.

Rekluz Labs designs, builds, tests, and maintains every application in-house — no studio-for-hire, no outsourced growth team, no ad network deciding what the product becomes.

Because one person is accountable for the whole product, technical decisions and ethical ones stay aligned instead of getting negotiated apart in a roadmap meeting. That shows up in concrete engineering choices, not just a policy page:

  • Native Android developmentKotlin
  • Processing kept on-device wherever practicalOffline-first
  • No advertising networks or behavioral trackersZero telemetry
  • Only the permissions a feature actually needsMinimal scope
  • Long-term maintenance over rapid re-releasesSustained support
Core Principles

What every release is checked against.

Privacy First

Your data belongs to you. Applications collect only what a feature strictly requires, and nothing is sold or shared.

Quality Over Quantity

Rather than shipping many rushed products, each application is deliberately designed and continually refined.

User Respect

No manipulative advertising, no deceptive subscriptions, no dark patterns — communication stays direct and clear.

Built to Last

Long-term support, regular updates, and development practices that hold up rather than accumulate debt.

Values
"Rekluz Labs is committed to building applications that are private by architecture, not by policy."

All applications are distributed free of charge, with no advertisements, subscriptions, or in-app purchases — a deliberate rejection of the incentives that prioritize engagement over user benefit. Each product is tested against real-world conditions and documented transparently, with technical and ethical decisions made by the same person, at the same time.

Development Philosophy

How it gets built, not just what ships.

Most indie developers don't talk about their process — Rekluz Labs treats it as part of the product. Every application is designed with maintainability and longevity as first-class requirements, not an afterthought once something breaks.

Technical decisions and their tradeoffs are documented as they're made, and tested against real-world conditions rather than abstract benchmarks. Thoughtful design and sound engineering are treated as the same discipline, expressed at different layers of the same product.

  • Native Android development
  • Modern Material Design
  • Accessibility as a default
  • Performance optimization
  • Battery efficiency
  • Reliable, ongoing updates
  • Continuous refinement
  • Transparent tradeoffs
