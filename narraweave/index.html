Serial fiction, generated with a key only you hold.

NarraWeave is a personalized serial fiction platform. You choose the AI provider, you bring the key, and your stories are generated directly through that provider's API and never through Rekluz Labs servers.

Check below for the PlayStore Link· Android

Bring your own key

Add an API key from any supported provider directly in the app. It's stored on-device and used only to talk to that provider, not to us.

Serial, not one-shot

Stories unfold in ongoing installments shaped by your choices, rather than a single generated block of text.

No middleman infrastructure

Requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. Rekluz Labs never sees your prompts, your key, or your stories.

Why BYOK
"You control the model, the cost, and where your data goes. We just build the app around that choice."

Most AI apps route every request through the developer's own backend, which means the developer sees what you write and decides what it costs. NarraWeave's bring-your-own-key model exists because that tradeoff shouldn't be the default, and it's a direct extension of the studio's mission statement.
